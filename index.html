Treatments For Better Recovery

Each treatment plan is selected around your pain pattern, mobility goals, and recovery stage.

Helps reduce stiffness and improve tissue mobility

Myofascial Release Therapy

Hands-on soft-tissue therapy designed to release restrictions, ease pain, and support smoother movement.

Aims to support sleep and mental balance

IMF Therapy

Individualized Microcurrent Frequencies (IMF) to harmonize the body's bioenergetic field, aimed at enhancing wellbeing, vitality, and balance.

Targeted care for pain relief and mobility

Physiotherapy & Exercise Rehab

Structured therapeutic exercises and guided rehabilitation for spine, joint, post-injury, and post-surgery recovery.

Clinical Specialities: What We Treat

Back & Spine Pain

Sciatica, posture-related pain, stiffness, and chronic back concerns.

Neck & Shoulder Pain

Mobility limitations, headaches related to neck strain, and shoulder dysfunction.

Knee & Joint Pain

Joint stiffness, arthritis support, movement training, and pain management.

Sports Injuries

Recovery planning for sprains, strains, overuse injuries, and return to activity.

Post-Surgery Rehab

Progressive rehabilitation to rebuild strength, range of motion, and confidence.

Neurological Conditions

Supportive therapy for balance, movement control, weakness, and coordination.
